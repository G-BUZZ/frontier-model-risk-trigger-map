# Frontier AI Governance Direction

## Research Focus

This project explores how evidence-based methods can support governance decisions for frontier AI systems.

The focus is not predicting catastrophic outcomes, but improving the process by which institutions interpret uncertain information.

## Key Questions

Current research questions include:

- How can public evaluations of frontier models be structured?
- When does uncertainty itself justify additional oversight?
- How can technical evidence be translated into governance actions?
- How should independent evaluation limitations affect confidence?

## Interdisciplinary Approach

The project combines:

- AI safety concepts;
- security risk analysis;
- legal reasoning;
- open-source intelligence methods;
- policy research.

## Intended Contribution

The long-term goal is to contribute to frontier AI governance through reproducible analysis, transparent evidence tracking and interdisciplinary research.
