# Roadmap

## Current Status

The project currently provides:

- frontier model registry;
- source registry;
- evidence dataset;
- evaluation registry;
- evidence confidence assessment;
- risk trigger mapping;
- governance decision framework;
- automated governance reporting.

## Short Term

Planned improvements:

- expand documented model coverage;
- improve evaluation metadata;
- add additional independent assessment sources;
- refine evidence provenance tracking;
- improve machine-readable outputs.

## Medium Term

Future development:

- broader regulatory mapping;
- improved cross-model comparison methods;
- additional governance workflows;
- contributor review processes.

## Long Term

Research directions:

- longitudinal frontier model risk tracking;
- improved uncertainty quantification;
- integration with public-interest AI governance research.

## Principles

The project will continue to avoid:

- universal risk scores;
- unsupported rankings;
- unverified claims.

The focus remains reproducible evidence-based governance analysis.
